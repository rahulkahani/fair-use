# Recording the landing page

The public UI is a simple landing page for creators. Recording instructions are documentation, not part of the page. The old Reel address now redirects to the landing page.

For a short screen recording:

1. Show the headline and explain: “I built fair-use to help creators explore copyright questions with their AI assistant.”
2. Scroll to the three steps: download the instructions, add them to an AI chat, and bring the project context.
3. Show the example message. Explain that the skill helps organize facts and questions for professional legal review.
4. End on the download link and the educational limitation.

If demonstrating a real AI response, switch to an assistant with the full instructions loaded and show its actual output, including questions, uncertainties, and capability limits. The landing page itself does not execute a model or assess images. Do not imply that scrolling the page shows live analysis.

Use your screen recorder and your own narration. No autoplay or director controls are included in the public interface.
