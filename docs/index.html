The skill helps your assistant organize sources and permissions, explain relevant considerations, and identify missing facts. It can help you prepare a brief for professional legal review.

Use an assistant with image support to examine pictures and web access to check current sources. Capabilities vary. Adding the instructions to one chat does not install them in every future conversation.

And “fair use” means…?

In US copyright law, fair use can allow certain uses of copyrighted work without permission. It depends on the purpose, the nature of the source, how much is taken, and the market effect—considered together. It isn’t a label an image earns by looking different.

Read the Copyright Office’s explanation ↗

The skill focuses on US fair use, with Canadian and UK routing notes. Rules elsewhere differ. Permission or a suitable license may be a separate basis for using a work.

Educational support, not legal advice. This skill is not a substitute for a qualified lawyer. It cannot certify fair use, determine infringement, or give permission to publish or sell. AI responses may be wrong, incomplete, or outdated.

This page does not run AI or receive your artwork. When you share files with your chosen assistant, that service’s data policies apply. Using the skill does not create an attorney-client relationship; do not assume your AI chat is legally privileged.
