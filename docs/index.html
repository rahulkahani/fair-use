And “fair use” means…?

In US copyright law, fair use can allow certain uses of copyrighted work without permission. It depends on the purpose, the nature of the source, how much is taken, and the market effect—considered together. It isn’t a label an image earns by looking different.

Read the Copyright Office’s explanation ↗

The skill focuses on US fair use, with Canadian and UK routing notes. Rules elsewhere differ. Permission or a suitable license may be a separate basis for using a work.
