# Contributing

Keep the project focused on copyright-use assessment for creators. Favor changes backed by an observed failure or a specific primary authority.

For legal changes, include jurisdiction, source URL, relevant section or holding, date, the claim being changed, and the limits of the authority. Distinguish statutes, binding decisions, agency guidance, contracts, and commentary. Do not widen a case's holding or label a pending dispute settled.

For behavioral changes, provide a reproducible fictional or authorized example, the observed response, and the behavior that should improve. Do not post private artwork, identifying disputes, legal correspondence, or material you lack permission to share in public issues.

Keep `SKILL.md` concise. Put specialized detail in a linked reference. Update the source-check date only after actually checking the relevant sources. Regenerate the chat attachment and ZIP when preparing a release; the skill and references are canonical.

Run `python3 scripts/check.py` and `python3 scripts/package.py`. Exercise the relevant cases in `evals/cases.json` and record the host/model, date, supplied inputs, actual response, observed failures, and verification limits. A structural check or an AI reviewer is not attorney validation.

The project does not accept guaranteed-clearance claims, percentage-change rules, a weighted legal risk calculator, detection-evasion instructions, or content that treats US fair use as worldwide law.
