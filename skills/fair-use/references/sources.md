# Primary-source register

Last checked: 2026-09-07. The dates below describe this package's research, not a guarantee of continuing validity. Recheck applicable sources during assessments and verify the particular court issue, statutory section or license version used. Do not represent this register as an exhaustive review of AI litigation.

| Source | Authority / date | Supports | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [17 USC Chapter 1](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) | US statute, official Copyright Office text | §§102, 106, 107; protected subject matter, rights, fair use | Case law and actual facts determine application |
| [More Information on Fair Use](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html) | US Copyright Office guidance; checked 2026-09-07 | Four factors and contextual balancing | Guidance, not a court decision |
| [Fair Use FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html) | US Copyright Office guidance; checked 2026-09-07 | No fixed safe amount; adaptations and permission | General explanation |
| [Warhol v. Goldsmith](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/22pdf/21-869_87ad.pdf) | US Supreme Court, 2023-05-18 | First factor for the challenged commercial licensing use | Not a holding about every use of the artwork or every factor; distinguish majority from dissent |
| [Campbell v. Acuff-Rose](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep510/usrep510569/usrep510569.pdf) | US Supreme Court, 1994-03-07 | Parody, commerciality and contextual balancing | Reversed and remanded; not automatic protection for humor |
| [Harper & Row v. Nation](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep471/usrep471539/usrep471539.pdf) | US Supreme Court, 1985-05-20 | Qualitative taking and first-publication market on its facts | Read with current §107 and subsequent law |
| [AI Part 2 announcement](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html) | US Copyright Office, 2025-01-29 | Human expressive contributions and output copyrightability | Agency analysis of authorship, not a blanket infringement ruling |
| [Copyright and AI reports](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/) | US Copyright Office, evolving collection | Original reports and updates | Identify the particular report/version; training questions differ from output use |
| [Canadian Act s29](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-42/Section-29.html) | Official Canadian legislation; checked 2026-09-07 | Listed fair-dealing purposes | Other provisions and Canadian cases also matter |
| [Canadian Act s29.21](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-42/section-29.21.html) | Official Canadian legislation; checked 2026-09-07 | Conditional noncommercial user-generated-content exception | Multiple conditions; not a universal AI-remix exception |
| [CCH v. Law Society](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/2125/1/document.do) | Supreme Court of Canada, 2004 SCC 13 | Two-stage approach and contextual fairness considerations, paras48–60 | Starting point, not exhaustive current Canadian analysis |
| [UK exceptions guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/exceptions-to-copyright) | UK Intellectual Property Office; checked 2026-09-07 | Local exception routing, including photograph distinction | Read governing legislation and case law for application |
| [Canva content license](https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/) | First-party contract; checked 2026-09-07 | Current library-content terms | Inspect applicable version, asset and account; not copyright legislation |
| [Canva policy archives](https://www.canva.com/policies/policy-archives/) | First-party archived contracts; checked 2026-09-07 | Locate dated policy versions | Must still establish which agreement applies to the user's act |
| [Canva AI terms](https://www.canva.com/policies/ai-product-terms/) | First-party contract; checked 2026-09-07 | Inputs, outputs and incorporated licensed content | Allocation between parties does not clear third-party rights |
| [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Creative Commons, version 4.0; checked 2026-09-07 | Scope and conditions of this license | Deed summarizes legal code; licensor authority and other rights remain relevant |

No third-party artwork, legal opinions, or full source documents are bundled. Links identify research sources; external sites retain their own terms and rights. Original examples in this package are fictional.
